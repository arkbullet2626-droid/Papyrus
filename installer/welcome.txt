Papyrus - instant replay for your games

While the replay buffer is on, Papyrus keeps the last 30 seconds of your game in memory as
compressed video. Press Ctrl+Shift+F9 and those 30 seconds are saved as an .mp4.

After setup:
  - Open "Papyrus" from the Start menu.
  - Clips shows your clips. Click one to open it in the editor.
  - Settings has the replay buffer switch (it starts OFF), the hotkey, clip length and quality.
  - Closing the window does not stop recording: Papyrus stays in the tray near the clock.
    Right-click its icon to exit.
  - Clips are saved in your Videos\Papyrus folder.

Exit any older Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus).

This early version needs an NVIDIA graphics card with a current driver (570 or newer).
Papyrus makes no network connections and collects nothing.
