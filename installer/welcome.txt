Papyrus - instant replay for your games

While the replay buffer is on, Papyrus keeps the last 30 seconds of your main monitor in
memory as compressed video. Press Ctrl+Shift+F9 and those 30 seconds are saved as an .mp4.

After setup:
  - Open "Papyrus" from the Start menu. The replay buffer starts OFF.
  - Click the big button to turn the replay buffer on or off.
  - Tick "Always on" if you want Papyrus to start with Windows with the buffer already on.
  - Closing the window keeps Papyrus in the tray near the clock. Right-click its icon to exit.
  - Clips are saved in your Videos\Papyrus folder.

If an older Papyrus is still running in a black window, close that window before you continue.

This early version needs an NVIDIA graphics card with a current driver (570 or newer).
It has no sound yet. Papyrus makes no network connections and collects nothing.
