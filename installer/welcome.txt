Papyrus - instant replay for your games

Papyrus keeps the last 30 seconds of your main monitor in memory as compressed video.
Press Ctrl+Shift+F9 at any time and those 30 seconds are saved as an .mp4.

After setup:
  - Start "Papyrus" from the Start menu. A small black window opens - leave it running.
  - Press Ctrl+Shift+F9 in a game. High beep = clip saved.
  - Clips are saved in your Videos\Papyrus folder ("Papyrus clips folder" in the Start menu).
  - Press Ctrl+C in the black window (or close it) to stop.

This early version needs an NVIDIA graphics card with a current driver (570 or newer).
It has no sound, tray icon or settings window yet - those arrive in later versions.

Papyrus makes no network connections and collects nothing.
