First release with a setup wizard. Milestone 2 of 7.

**What it does**
- Keeps the last 30 seconds of the main monitor in RAM as compressed HEVC video and saves them to an `.mp4` when you press **Ctrl+Shift+F9**.
- Clips go to `Videos\Papyrus`.

**Needs**
- Windows 10 1903 or newer, NVIDIA graphics card, driver 570 or newer.

**Not yet**
- Audio, tray icon and notifications, settings window, HDR, AV1, AMD and Intel cards.

**Installing**
Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.2.2.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
