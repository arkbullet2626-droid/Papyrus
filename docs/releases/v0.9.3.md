Frame-exact cuts and independent sound tracks.

**Changed in the editor**
- **Smooth cutting:** the clip's bars now move one frame at a time instead of jumping a second. Times are shown to the millisecond. The picture is still copied without re-encoding.
- **Sound tracks stay put:** moving the clip's bars no longer moves the sound tracks' bars.
  - A track you have not touched is cut where the clip is cut ("Follows the clip").
  - Once you move a track's bars it plays exactly where they are ("Own range"), even before the picture starts or after it ends.
- **Snap kept:** a track's bar still snaps onto the clip's start or end when you drag it close, and can be dragged away again.
- **Reset** links every track to the clip again.

**Good to know**
- A frame-exact start works by telling the player where to begin. VLC, browsers and Discord follow that. A player that ignores it starts up to a second early, with picture and sound still together.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.3.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
