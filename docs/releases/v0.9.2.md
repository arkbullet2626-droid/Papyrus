Sound tracks follow the clip.

**Changed in the editor**
- **Tracks stay the clip's length:** when you drag the clip's bars, every sound track's bars move with them, so the tracks stay the same length as the clip.
- **Snap:** when you drag a sound track's bar close to the clip's start or end, it snaps onto it. You can still drag it away again.
- **Inside the clip:** a sound track's bars cannot go outside the clip's bars.
- A track you shortened on purpose keeps its own position when the clip's bars move, unless the clip is cut past it.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.2.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
