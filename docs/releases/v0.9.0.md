One Papyrus app: clips, editor and settings in a single window.

**New**
- **Papyrus is now one app** with navigation on the left. The separate Studio window and the small settings window are gone.
- **Clips page:** a grid of your clips with thumbnails, date, length and size. The folder they are saved in is shown at the top.
- **Hover preview:** rest the pointer on a clip for 2 seconds and it plays inside its card, silently. Move away and it stops.
- **Click a clip** to open it in the editor. "Back to clips" returns to the grid.
- **Settings page:** the replay buffer switch and status, Always on, what to record, save hotkey, clip length, frame rate, quality, microphone, and the clips location. Changes apply straight away.
- A dot in the title bar shows the recorder state: red recording, amber waiting, grey off.

**How it fits together**
- Closing the Papyrus window does not stop recording. The small recorder stays in the tray near the clock; click its icon to open Papyrus again, right-click to exit.
- Opening Papyrus starts the tray recorder if it is not already running.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.0.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
