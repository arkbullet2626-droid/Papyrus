Fullscreen-only mode keeps recording when you tab out.

**Changed**
- In **Fullscreen apps and games only** mode, Papyrus no longer pauses when you alt-tab away from a game. As long as the fullscreen app is still running, the replay buffer keeps recording whatever is on the main monitor.
- A fullscreen app is now detected even if it is behind other windows when you switch the buffer on.
- The buffer pauses only when the fullscreen app closes or leaves fullscreen. What was recorded stays available for 5 minutes, then the recorder shuts down and frees its memory.

**Also**
- The installer now includes the MIT license.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.5.1.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
