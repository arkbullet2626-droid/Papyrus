Fixes found by testing the mix option on a real PC.

**Fixed**
- **Mixed track was 21 ms early:** a clip saved with the mix option had its sound about 21 milliseconds ahead of the picture. Fixed.
- **Recorded sound was 21 ms early too:** the same wrong assumption about the Windows audio encoder affected every newly recorded clip. New clips are now stamped correctly. Clips recorded before this version keep the small offset.
- **Volume changes** in the editor had the same 21 ms offset. Fixed.
- **Mix could distort:** when the tracks together were too loud, the peaks were cut off. The mix is now turned down just enough to fit.
- **Old message stayed on screen:** the green "Saved" bar no longer follows you to another page.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.7.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
