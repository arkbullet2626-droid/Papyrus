Settings in the control window.

**New**
- **Save hotkey:** click the box and press the key combination you want.
- **Clip length:** 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 1.5 minutes or 2 minutes.
- **Frame rate:** 120, 60 or 30 fps.
- **Quality:** 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p or 480p. Clips are scaled down on the graphics card and never scaled up, so a setting above your screen's resolution records at the screen's own size.
- The window shows the resolution, bitrate and estimated memory use for the current settings before you turn the buffer on.

**Fixed**
- The help text at the bottom of the window was cut off.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.4.0.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
