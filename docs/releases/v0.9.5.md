Mix the sound tracks into one for sharing.

**New in the editor**
- **Mix into one track:** tick "When saving, mix the kept tracks into one track" and the saved clip has a single sound track called "Mixed" with everything you kept: game, Spotify, Discord and microphone. Every player, and Discord, plays all of it.
- The mix respects each track's own bars, Keep box and volume.
- The choice is remembered for next time. It is off until you tick it, and the original clip always keeps its separate tracks.

**Good to know**
- Mixing re-encodes the sound once (the picture is still copied untouched), so saving takes a little longer.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.5.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
