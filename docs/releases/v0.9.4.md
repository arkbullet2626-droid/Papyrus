Black screen where there is sound but no picture.

**Changed**
- **Black screen for sound-only parts:** when a sound track starts before the picture or continues after it, the saved clip now shows black there instead of a frozen frame. The black frames are made by the graphics card's encoder when you save.
- **Frame-exact cuts work in every player:** the lead-in frames before a cut are now given a display time of practically zero, so a clip starts on the frame you chose without the player needing any special support.

**Good to know**
- Saving a clip that needs black frames takes a moment longer and needs the NVIDIA card.
- Clips that do not need black frames are saved exactly as before.

**Installing**
Exit Papyrus first (right-click its tray icon, Exit Papyrus). Download `Papyrus-Setup-0.9.4.exe` below and run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen will show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
